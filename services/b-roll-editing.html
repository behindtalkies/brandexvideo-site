B-Roll Editing Service

B-roll is the footage that plays over your voice — the hands typing, the clinic doorway, the product on the desk. Cut well, it doubles retention. BrandexVideo's b-roll editing service syncs cinematic b-roll into your talking-head videos, colour grades it to match, and delivers platform-ready cuts in 24–48 hours.

Why b-roll is the difference between 200 views and 20,000

A talking head on its own is a static frame for 60 seconds. The viewer's eye has nothing new to land on, and the 10-second retention cliff hits hard. B-roll fixes that. Every 3–5 seconds a new image appears — your hands, your workspace, a product, a movement — and attention resets.

The catch: b-roll only works when it's cut to the speech. An insert that lands mid-sentence looks random. One that lands on the key word feels intentional. That timing is the craft, and it's what our editors do all day.

Frequently asked questions

What is b-roll editing?

B-roll editing is cutting supplementary footage — hands, environment, product, movement — over a primary talking-head track so the viewer sees action while hearing the message. Good b-roll editing matches the cut to the beat of the speech and grades both sources to one consistent look.

Do I need to shoot b-roll myself?

No. Send us your talking-head footage and any clips you have. We select, cut and grade b-roll from your footage. If you have none, we brief you on 5–8 simple b-roll shots to capture on your phone in 10 minutes.

How much b-roll should a 60-second video have?

Typically 30–50% of screen time. A b-roll insert every 3–5 seconds acts as a pattern interrupt and holds retention past the 10-second drop-off most short-form videos suffer.
