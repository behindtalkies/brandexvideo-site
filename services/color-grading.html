Video Colour Grading Service

Colour is why two videos of the same person can look like a phone clip and a film. Our colour grading service corrects exposure and skin tones, applies a consistent brand look across every video you publish, and — for the footage most creators actually have — rescues phone clips shot in bad light. Included in every editing plan.

Why grading matters more on social than anywhere else

Your videos are viewed in a feed, next to hundreds of others. The one that looks graded — consistent, warm skin, controlled highlights — reads as professional before the viewer registers a word. The one that looks like a raw phone clip reads as amateur, even if the content is better. And across a grid or profile, consistency is what makes a feed look like a brand instead of a camera roll.

Choosing your brand look

On your first batch we send two or three graded versions of the same clip — for example warm and soft, clean and bright, cinematic and contrasty. You pick one. That becomes your grade for every video after. If you already have a visual identity or a reference creator whose look you like, send it and we match to that.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between colour correction and colour grading?

Correction fixes problems — exposure, white balance, skin tone — so footage looks accurate. Grading adds a look — warmth, contrast, a colour palette — so footage looks intentional and consistent. We do both on every video.

Can you fix phone footage shot in bad lighting?

Usually, yes. Mixed light sources, orange indoor tint, blown-out windows and underexposed faces are the most common issues in creator footage and all are correctable to a point. Very underexposed footage will look grainy — we'll tell you if a reshoot would be better.

Will all my videos look the same?

Consistent, yes — identical, no. We build a grade for your brand (warm and soft, clean and bright, cinematic and contrasty) and apply it to every video so your feed looks like one creator. Individual clips are corrected first so the grade sits right on each.

Do you create a LUT I can use myself?

Yes, on request. Once your brand grade is set, we export it as a LUT file you can apply in CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci for anything you edit yourself.
