Instagram Reels Editing Service

Instagram's algorithm rewards completion rate above everything else. Our Reels editing service cuts for that: hook in frame one, a visual change every 3–5 seconds, captions inside the safe zone, and a hard cut at the end so the loop lands clean. First cut in 24–48 hours, published to your account.

What makes a Reel perform

Instagram's ranking for Reels is driven by watch time and completion rate, then shares and saves. That means the edit has three jobs: stop the scroll in under 2 seconds, hold attention past the 10-second cliff, and end before the viewer decides to leave. Everything we do serves those three.

Reels vs. the same edit on other platforms

All three short-form platforms use a 1080×1920 canvas, but Instagram blocks the bottom 310px and top 210px with its interface. We edit one master inside the universal 900×1400px safe zone, then nudge captions 40px up for the Reels export. One edit, three platform files. Full breakdown here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best length for an Instagram Reel?

20–40 seconds for most content. Instagram allows up to 3 minutes, but completion rate is the strongest ranking signal and shorter Reels complete more often. We cut to one idea, fully delivered, and stop.

What is the Instagram Reels safe zone?

Keep text and faces away from the bottom 310px and top 210px of the 1080×1920 frame — that's where the caption, audio track, buttons and status bar sit. We use a 900×1400px centred box that also clears TikTok and Shorts.

Do you publish the Reels for me?

Yes. After you approve the cut, we schedule it through Meta Business Suite with a platform-optimised caption, hashtags and a cover frame, posted at your audience's peak time. You share access — never your password.

Can I use the same edit for TikTok and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. We export one master with all elements inside the universal safe zone, then adjust captions and audio per platform. You get three platform-ready files from one edit.
