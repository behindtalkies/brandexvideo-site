The Ideal Instagram Reel Length in 2026, by Goal

✍️ Shankar, Creative Director📅 9 November 2026⏱ 6 min read

The ideal length for Instagram Reels in 2026 is 30–60 seconds for most business accounts. In Socialinsider's analysis of about 9.8 million brand Reels posted between January and June 2026, the 45–60 second bracket had both the highest median views and the highest engagement rate. Go shorter (15–30 seconds) when the goal is pure reach, longer (60–90 seconds) when you are teaching or selling, and stay under 3 minutes.

There is no single right length, because a Reel made to reach strangers and a Reel made to convince a buyer do different jobs. This guide covers what the data shows, what Instagram allows, and the length to aim for with each goal.

What does the 2026 data say about Reel length?

The largest recent dataset is Socialinsider's study of roughly 9.8 million organic Reels from brand accounts, posted from January to June 2026. It grouped Reels by duration and compared median views and engagement rate.

  • Under 30 seconds: 4,700 median views, 0.28% engagement rate.
  • 30–45 seconds: 8,564 median views, 0.30%.
  • 45–60 seconds: 10,374 median views, 0.35%. The best bracket on both measures.
  • 60–90 seconds: 9,790 median views, 0.30%.
  • 90 seconds to 3 minutes: 8,000–9,000 median views, 0.30–0.33%.
  • Over 3 minutes: 4,428 median views, 0.15%. The worst bracket on both.

Two cautions apply. This is correlation: accounts that make 50-second Reels may simply plan their videos better. And the engagement rate counts likes and comments against followers, so it says nothing about saves, shares or sales.

The safe conclusion is narrower than "longer is better". The very short Reel is no longer the automatic winner, and anything from 30 to 90 seconds can perform when it holds attention.

How long should a Reel be for each goal?

These are the working ranges we edit to. They are judgment built on the data above and on what we see across client accounts, not platform rules.

GoalIdeal lengthWhat fits in it
Reach and new followers15–30 secondsOne hook, one point, an ending that loops
Authority and education30–60 secondsOne idea explained: hook, problem, answer
Trust and sales60–90 secondsA case study, a process, an objection answered
Depth and storytelling90 seconds–3 minutesOnly when every section earns its place
Anything over 3 minutesAvoid for growthNot recommended to new audiences

Most service businesses, such as clinics, law firms, advisers and coaches, need the middle rows far more than the first. A 20-second trend clip may collect views, but a 50-second answer to a real client question is what makes a stranger book a call.

The editing style matters too. A video in the bold caption style, built on one strong opinion, is at its best at 20–45 seconds. A calm explainer needs closer to a minute.

How long can an Instagram Reel be in 2026?

Instagram's Help Center says Reels can be recorded and edited up to 20 minutes. The limit that matters for growth is much lower: the same guidance states that Reels over 3 minutes will not be recommended to new audiences.

That ceiling is recent. Reels were limited to 90 seconds until January 2025, when Instagram extended them to 3 minutes. Treat 3 minutes as the edge of discovery, not as a target.

Do shorter Reels get more reach?

Not automatically. Instagram's ranking leans on how much of a Reel people watch, and on whether they share and save it. A short video is easier to finish, which helps completion. It also gives the viewer less time to care enough to share it.

A 15-second Reel that says nothing loses to a 55-second Reel that people watch to the end. The reverse is also true: a 55-second Reel with 20 seconds of warm-up loses to the tight version of itself.

So the practical rule is to make the Reel as long as the idea needs and not a second longer. Length is a result of the edit, not a setting you choose first.

A quick way to choose a length

Write the one sentence the viewer should remember. If one example proves it, aim for 30–45 seconds. If it needs a story or three steps, aim for 60–90 seconds. If it needs more than that, it is two Reels.

How do you edit a Reel down to the right length?

Most raw clips run well past their ideal length, and the extra is rarely in the content. It is in the warm-up, the pauses and the repetition. Five cuts remove it.

  • Start on the hook. Cut the greeting, the introduction and the context. The first 3 seconds decide whether anyone sees the rest, as our anatomy of a 60-second video explains.
  • Remove breaths and dead air. Tightening the gaps between sentences shortens a video without losing a single word.
  • Keep one idea per Reel. A second point belongs in a second video, which also gives you more to post.
  • Cut the recap. End on the answer or the call to action. Nobody needs a summary of a 45-second video.
  • Change the picture every 3–5 seconds. A punch-in, a b-roll insert or a caption beat makes a 60-second Reel feel shorter than a static 30-second one.

Frequently asked questions

What is the ideal length for an Instagram Reel in 2026?

For most business accounts the ideal length is 30–60 seconds. In Socialinsider's analysis of about 9.8 million brand Reels from January to June 2026, Reels of 45–60 seconds had the highest median views and engagement rate.

How long can Instagram Reels be?

Instagram's Help Center says Reels can be recorded and edited up to 20 minutes, but Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to new audiences. For growth, treat 3 minutes as the practical maximum.

Do shorter Reels get more views?

Not necessarily. In Socialinsider's 2026 data, Reels under 30 seconds had lower median views than Reels of 45–60 seconds. A Reel performs when people watch it to the end, whatever its length.

Is 90 seconds too long for a Reel?

No. Reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended to new audiences, and 60–90 seconds suits case studies, processes and client stories. It is too long only if the video has warm-up or repetition that could be cut.

How long should a Reel be for a business?

Use 15–30 seconds for reach, 30–60 seconds to teach one idea and build authority, and 60–90 seconds for case studies and sales content. Most service businesses get the most from the 30–60 second range.

Should every Reel be the same length?

No. Match the length to the job of each video: short for reach, longer for explanation and trust. A mix of lengths across the week is normal.
