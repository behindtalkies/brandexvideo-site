How Remote Video Editing Works: Sending Footage, Reviewing Cuts, Publishing From Anywhere

✍️ Shankar, Creative Director📅 4 September 2026⏱ 5 min read

Remote video editing runs on three things: a shared Google Drive folder for footage, timestamped comments for revisions, and direct publishing to your accounts. Upload at night, wake up to a finished cut. Here's the complete workflow we use with clients in the USA, UK and UAE from our studio in India.

The most common question we get from first-time clients isn't about price or quality — it's "how does this actually work if you're 10 hours away?" The answer is that distance is irrelevant once the system is set up. This is the system.

Step 1: The folder

We create a Google Drive folder for you with three sub-folders: Raw, Review, Final. You get edit access. That's the entire infrastructure.

  • Raw — you upload footage here, straight from your phone or camera
  • Review — we drop cuts here for you to check
  • Final — approved videos, exported per platform

No WeTransfer links that expire, no email size limits, no Dropbox requests. Files sync automatically and everything stays in one place.

Step 2: What to film

You don't need a camera. A phone from the last three years shoots better video than most cameras from five years ago. What matters:

SettingDo thisWhy
OrientationVertical (9:16)Cropping horizontal to vertical loses 60% of the frame
Resolution4K if the phone supports itGives room to punch in without going soft
FramingEyes in the top third, centredKeeps your face inside every platform's safe zone
AudioQuiet room, phone within 1m, or a $20 lapel micBad audio is the #1 reason viewers scroll — more than bad video
LightFace a window, no window behind youFront light flatters; backlight silhouettes
StructureOne question per clip, say the question firstLets us cut clean without hunting for the start

Leave 2 seconds of silence at the start and end of each clip. It costs nothing and makes every edit cleaner.

Step 3: File naming

This sounds trivial. It saves hours. Name clips like this:

2026-09-04_Q03_why-does-my-knee-click.mov

Date, question number, short description. We know exactly what's in it before opening it, and we can match it to your question list.

Step 4: The edit

Your editor picks up the Raw folder, cuts to the structure in our 60-second anatomy, grades to your brand colours, adds captions and music, exports for each platform, and drops the cut in Review. Starter plan: 48 hours. Growth: 24. Scale: 12.

Step 5: Reviewing remotely

Watch the cut. If it's right, say so. If not, comment with timestamps — in the Drive file's comment panel, on WhatsApp, or by email:

  • "0:14 — cut the pause before 'actually'"
  • "0:32 — caption says 'their', should be 'there'"
  • "0:48 — can the CTA be bigger?"

Timestamps are the whole trick. "Make it punchier" takes three rounds. "0:14 cut the pause" takes one. Revised cut comes back the same day, usually.

Step 6: Publishing

Once approved, we schedule and post directly to your accounts. You share access through Meta Business Suite or a scheduling tool — never your password. We write the platform caption, add hashtags, pick the peak posting time for your audience's time zone, and post. You get a notification when it's live.

Why time zones help, not hurt

Our clients in New York upload at 9pm. Our editors in Chennai start at 9am — which is 11:30pm New York time. The cut is in the Review folder by the time the client's morning coffee is ready. The 9.5-hour gap means work happens while you sleep. Same-timezone editors can't offer that.

The only real requirement is that both sides respond within a business day. Everything else is handled by the folder.

Frequently asked questions

How do I send large video files to a remote editor?

Through a shared Google Drive folder. Your editor creates the folder, you upload directly from your phone or laptop, and files sync automatically. No WeTransfer links that expire, no email limits.

What should I film for a remote editor?

Film vertical 9:16 if the final is for Reels or TikTok, in 4K if possible, with the subject centred. Record each answer as a separate clip, say the question first, and leave 2 seconds of silence before and after. Good audio matters more than good video.

How do revisions work remotely?

You watch the delivered cut, leave comments with timestamps — '0:14 remove the pause', '0:32 caption typo' — and the editor returns a revised version, usually the same day. Unlimited rounds are included at BrandexVideo.

Does the time difference matter for remote editing?

No. Remote editing is asynchronous: you upload at night, the editor works during their day, the cut is waiting when you wake up. A 9–12 hour time difference often speeds things up because work happens while you sleep.
