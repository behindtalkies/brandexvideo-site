How Much Does It Cost to Edit a TikTok Video in 2026?

✍️ Shankar, Creative Director📅 13 October 2026⏱ 6 min read

Editing a single TikTok video costs $25–75 from a beginner freelancer, $75–200 from an experienced one, and $70–80 per video inside a monthly plan that covers 10 or more videos. Agencies quote $300 and up per clip. The spread comes down to four things: footage quality, turnaround, graphics and how many videos you need each month.

Search for a TikTok editor and you'll see quotes from $20 to $500 for what looks like the same 45-second clip. The range is real, and it isn't random. This guide lays out the 2026 rates for each way of buying TikTok editing, what you get at each tier, and the point where paying per video stops making sense.

Hourly pricing exists too — freelance editors charge roughly $25–150 an hour in 2026 — but for short-form work per-video pricing is far more common, because a 45-second TikTok can take anywhere from one to six hours depending on the brief.

What you actually get at each price

$20–75: template editing

Your clip is dropped into a preset. You get trimmed footage, auto-generated captions and possibly a trending sound. No colour grading, no hook engineering, usually a single revision. It's fine for testing whether TikTok is worth your time at all; the inconsistency shows the moment you post three times a week.

$75–200: professional editing

This is where retention-focused editing starts. Expect a deliberate first-three-second hook, pacing cuts every 2–4 seconds, custom animated captions, licensed music and a couple of revision rounds. Rush delivery usually adds 20–40% on top.

$200–500: specialist work

Motion graphics, product call-outs, multi-layer sound design and brand-consistent colour grading. Worth it for a hero piece or a paid campaign; overkill for a daily talking-head clip.

$70–80 per video: monthly plans

The four things that move the price

  1. Footage quality. Clean phone footage with good light is quick to cut. Shaky, under-lit footage with poor audio adds cleanup time, and editors price that in. Our guide to editing phone videos covers what to send.
  2. Turnaround. Standard is 48–72 hours on marketplaces. 24-hour delivery is the most common surcharge in 2026, typically 20–40%. Monthly plans usually include it.
  3. Graphics and grading. Motion titles, lower thirds, product pop-ups and grading can double or triple a per-video freelance quote. They're the first thing to check when two quotes look very different.
  4. Volume. One video is a project; twenty is an operation. Per-video pricing is efficient up to about five videos a month and inefficient after that.
Ask what counts as one video

Most editors and plans define one video as up to 60 seconds of finished output. A 90-second TikTok is two videos. If a quote doesn't say, ask before you send footage — it's the most common cause of surprise invoices.

How to get an accurate quote in one message

Editors quote high when the brief is vague. Send these five things and the number you get back will be the number you pay:

  • Length of raw footage and how it was shot (phone, mirrorless, screen recording)
  • Finished length you want (most TikToks that perform are 21–45 seconds)
  • Caption style, with one example link
  • Turnaround you need
  • How many videos per month, so the editor can price volume

Frequently asked questions

How much should I pay to edit a 30-second TikTok?

In 2026, $25–75 from a beginner, $75–200 from an experienced freelancer, or $70–80 per video inside a monthly plan of 10 or more videos. Length under 60 seconds rarely changes the price; the brief and graphics do.

Why is one editor $20 and another $300 for the same TikTok?

The $20 edit is template cutting with auto-captions and one revision. The $300 edit includes hook engineering, custom animated captions, colour grading, motion graphics and sound design. You are paying for the process, not the minutes of footage.

Does rush delivery cost extra for TikTok editing?

Usually yes. 24-hour delivery is the most common surcharge in 2026, typically 20–40% on freelance quotes. Monthly editing plans normally include 24-hour turnaround at no extra cost.

Is a monthly editing plan cheaper than a freelancer?

Once you post more than about five TikToks a month, yes. A $799 plan for 10 videos is $80 per video with grading, captions and unlimited revisions included, which freelancers generally bill as extras.

What counts as one video in TikTok editing pricing?

Most editors and plans define one video as up to 60 seconds of finished output. A 90-second TikTok counts as two videos. Confirm this before sending footage.
