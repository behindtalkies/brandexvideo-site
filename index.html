FAQ

Questions we
hear often

How do I send you my footage?
+
You send footage to BrandexVideo through a shared Google Drive folder we create for you. Upload raw clips from your phone or camera — no special software needed. Our editor downloads them, edits the video and returns a finished cut within 24–48 hours. This works for clients anywhere in the world.
What if I don't like the edit?
+
Every BrandexVideo plan includes unlimited revisions at no extra charge. You leave timestamped comments on the delivered video, and our editor revises until you approve it. Most revisions are returned the same day.
How fast is the turnaround?
+
Essentials: 48 hours. Growth and Scale plans: 24 hours. Scale clients get priority 12-hour turnaround. We work across time zones to make sure deadlines are always met.
Do you publish to my social media accounts?
+
Yes. We post directly to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and Facebook. Each video is reformatted and optimised specifically for the platform — not just the same file reposted.
Can I pause or cancel anytime?
+
Absolutely. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Pause for a month if you're travelling or cancel anytime. We make it easy to come back whenever you're ready.
What countries do you serve?
+
We are fully remote and serve clients globally — USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia and beyond. All communication is async-first via email, WhatsApp and shared Drive.
