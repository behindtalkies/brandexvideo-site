Frequently asked questions

How much recording does a coach need to do?

One 60–90 minute session a month, on a phone, talking through 10–15 ideas from a list we send you. That covers 4 weeks of posting on the Starter or Growth plan.

Do you write the scripts?

No — we send a prompt list and you talk naturally. Your voice is the product. We edit for structure: hook, idea, example, takeaway.

What's the best CTA for coaches?

Soft and consistent: 'DM me the word GROWTH' or 'link in bio for the free guide'. Hard sells in short-form get skipped. We add your CTA as an end card and in the caption.

Can you edit long-form course lessons into Reels?

Yes — send the lesson, we pull the 5–8 strongest 45-second moments and edit each as a standalone Reel with its own hook.

Send us your footage. We'll handle the rest.

Upload to a shared Drive folder, first cut in 24–48 hours, unlimited revisions, published to your accounts.
