Frequently asked questions

What content gets consultants inbound leads?

Specific, opinionated, and repeated. 'Why most SaaS pricing pages fail' from a pricing consultant generates DMs. Generic 'leadership tips' does not.

How is LinkedIn video edited differently?

Square or 4:5, larger captions for desktop, text hook on frame one (LinkedIn autoplays muted), slower pacing, no trending audio. We export LinkedIn and Instagram versions from one edit.

Can you add framework graphics?

Yes — simple overlay diagrams, numbered steps and highlighted text in your brand style. Included on Growth and Scale.

Do I need to be on camera?

Yes for this to work. Consultants are hired as people. A face and a clear point of view is the product; we make it look considered.
