Frequently asked questions

How do you keep ink colours accurate?

Skin-tone-first grading — we correct the skin to natural and let the ink sit true against it. Studio lighting is often cool; we warm it without shifting the ink.

Can you handle platform nudity rules?

Yes — we crop and frame to stay inside guidelines. Chest, thigh and back pieces need careful framing; we flag anything that risks a takedown.

Do you edit time-lapse footage?

Yes — speed-ramped process with normal-speed moments on the wipe and the reveal, cut to the beat.

What tattoo content books clients?

Healed reveals. Fresh tattoos all look good; healed ones prove your work. Pair each with the process clip.
