Frequently asked questions

Do you edit medical content responsibly?

We edit what you say — we never add claims, statistics or advice. Captions are transcribed word-for-word and checked against your speech. If a clip contains a claim you'd rather not publish, we cut it and tell you.

How much time does a doctor need to spend?

About 90 minutes a month: 60 minutes recording 10–12 answers on a phone, 30 minutes reviewing edits. We send the question list a week ahead.

Which platform should a doctor use?

Instagram for most specialities, LinkedIn for referral-driven practices, YouTube Shorts for procedure-heavy fields like dermatology. Start with one; we add the others once the first is consistent.

Can you anonymise patient data in footage?

Yes. We blur screens, documents and faces on request, and we flag anything visible in the background that looks like patient information before publishing.
